[h2]What this patch does[/h2]
One entry: [i]ai_strategy_default[/i], the strategy every AI country loads before its own.

[list]
[*]The Great Revision writes it with three [i]INJECT:[/i] files — twelve institutions of its own for the AI to invest in, a 10 → 500 weight on vanilla's police, health system and home affairs, three naval unit-group weights, a higher conscription ratio under National Militia, and its own list of diplomatic-play scenarios for the German and Italian unification wars.
[*]Kuromi's AI then ships its whole default strategy as a bare body at the vanilla path [i]common/ai_strategies/00_default_strategy.txt[/i], and a bare body in a later mod eats every injection that came before it. All three TGR files stop working the moment KAI is installed.
[*]There is no error for this. An injection that lands under a bare body is not a missing key — it is simply never applied, and the AI plays without it.
[/list]

This patch re-issues TGR's block on top of whatever body wins, sub-block by sub-block. [i]diplomatic_play_support[/i] is merged: the winning list minus its German Leadership War block (TGR ships its own version of the same scenario and the two would add up), plus TGR's scenarios. [i]wanted_construction_output[/i] is left to Kuromi's AI — both authors rewrote the formula whole, the later mod owns it, and this is an AI mod's own subject.

[h2]Checked and deliberately not patched[/h2]
Of the 37 keys the two mods share, this is the only one that needs a file. The rest, with the reason, is in [i]conflicts_tgr_vs_kai_report.md[/i]:
[list]
[*][b]24 laws[/b] — every one of KAI's entries is an [i]INJECT:[/i], almost always of [i]ai_enact_weight_modifier[/i] alone. TGR's law bodies stay intact.
[*][b]building_construction_sector[/b], [b]foreign_investment_rights[/b] — TGR writes the body, KAI injects one sub-block. Merges by itself.
[*][b]atmospheric_engine[/b], [b]mechanical_tools[/b] — both sides inject, into different sub-blocks.
[*][b]NAI[/b] — defines merge per key. The eight keys that really overlap are all [i]MONEY_SPENDING_*[/i] thresholds; both authors set them on purpose and KAI wins by load order. Picking a set would be a rebalance.
[*][b]wanted_army_size_script_value[/b] — both authors rewrote vanilla's formula whole and both open with [i]value = 0[/i], so whoever is last owns it outright. That is Kuromi's AI, and army sizing is an AI mod's own subject. TGR's larger great-power bonus (+100/+50 against KAI's +25/+10) and its [i]fixed_income[/i] term are not in the game with both mods on. Deliberate, not a break. [i](Until 25.08.2026 Tech & Res was the last writer here; with it gone the winner is KAI.)[/i]
[*][b]Six other AI strategies[/b] — agricultural expansion, colonial extraction, industrial expansion, placate population, plantation economy, resource expansion. Both authors write complete, deliberately different strategies; KAI is later and wins. Merging them would be a rebalance.
[/list]
