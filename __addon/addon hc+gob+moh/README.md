# Addon 1 — Hail, Columbia! + Gates of the Bosphorus + Mandate of Heaven × MegaComPatch (+ Victorian Century)

Built 24.08.2026 for game 1.13 (exe 1.13.11). Rebuilt 25.08.2026 without Tech & Res. Extended 26.08.2026 with the HC+GoB+MoH × Victorian Century pair.

This is the first of the addon layers that sit on top of the MegaComPatch. The megapack covers its own blocks; each new block added above it needs one layer that closes that block against everything below. This is the layer for **Hail, Columbia! + Gates of the Bosphorus + Mandate of Heaven** — including its compatibility with **Victorian Century**, if you use it.

It is a merge of four pair compatches kept separately in the repository:

| compatch | pair |
| --- | --- |
| `_HC+GoB+MoH/hc+morg done` | HC + GoB + MoH × Morgenröte |
| `_HC+GoB+MoH/hc+tgr done` | HC + GoB + MoH × The Great Revision (also carries its Victorian Century layer — see below) |
| `_HC+GoB+MoH/hc+kai done` | HC + GoB + MoH × Kuromi's AI |
| `_HC+GoB+MoH/hc+vc done` | HC + GoB + MoH × Victorian Century |

Every file is generated by `tools/regen_addon1.py` (the first three pairs) or `tools/regen_hc_vc.py` (the fourth) out of the mods themselves, and assembled by `tools/build_addon1.py`. Nothing here is hand-edited.

**Tech & Res left this set on 25.08.2026.** Four files went with it: the four slavery laws, the Greener Grass decree, `je_warlord_china` and T&R's re-issued `ai_strategy_default` injection. Each of them was a merge of a T&R body with an HC or MoH one, so without T&R they would install content from a mod you do not have — and `je_warlord_china` would point `on_complete` at `the_future_of_china.201`, an event that no longer exists. They are kept as sources in `_HC+GoB+MoH/hc+kai done/_to_delete/tr_removed_2026-08-25/`.

```
>>> this addon <<<
```

Three ordering requirements hold whether or not this addon is installed, and the conclusions below depend on them:

* **Mandate of Heaven after Hail, Columbia!** — otherwise MoH's `INJECT:` into `ig_rural_folk` lands under HC's bare `00_rural_folk.txt` and disappears.
* **LLWA, when you add it, after Mandate of Heaven** — otherwise LLWA's `INJECT: subsidies` lands under MoH's `REPLACE:ai_strategy_default` and disappears.
* **Victorian Century, if you use it, before Hail, Columbia!** — VC ships 55+ files at vanilla paths with bare bodies, including the Chinese and Ottoman history files and all eight interest groups. Loaded after this block it eats the merges below.

**Victorian Century is optional.** Unlike Hail, Columbia! / Gates of the Bosphorus / Mandate of Heaven themselves, this addon works correctly whether or not you run Victorian Century — see "Playing without Victorian Century" below for the exact files to remove if you don't.

## What this addon does

### Kuromi's AI comes back to life

`ai_strategy_default` is the entry every AI country loads before its own strategy. Kuromi's AI rewrites it (42 changed passages, shipped at the vanilla path `common/ai_strategies/00_default_strategy.txt`), and Mandate of Heaven then `REPLACE:`s it with a 9075-line body built on vanilla — which drops the lot. An AI mod being switched off by a flavour mod produces no error: the AI simply plays vanilla again.

The addon ships a three-way merge of Kuromi's AI and Mandate of Heaven against vanilla as the common base, plus The Great Revision's injection into the same entry re-issued in its own file on top of it.

### The Great Revision's AI injections come back as well

The Great Revision writes the same entry with three `INJECT:` files — twelve institutions of its own for the AI to invest in, three naval unit-group weights, a higher conscription ratio under National Militia, and its own list of diplomatic-play scenarios for the German and Italian unification wars. Kuromi's AI erases all three before this addon is in the picture, which is the TGR × Kuromi pair and is fixed in the megapack; Mandate of Heaven and the merged body above then drop that fix too. So the same block is re-issued here, built against the merged body.

Two of the five sub-blocks are also written by Kuromi's AI. `diplomatic_play_support` is merged — the winning list minus its German Leadership War block, which TGR ships its own version of, plus TGR's scenarios. `wanted_construction_output` is left to Kuromi's AI: both authors rewrote the formula whole, and the later mod owns it.

### The Great Revision's interest groups come back — and now Victorian Century's rework of them too

Hail, Columbia! ships `common/interest_groups/00_landowners.txt` and `00_rural_folk.txt` at the vanilla paths, with bare bodies, and loads after TGR (and after Victorian Century) — so both TGR's and VC's versions of both groups are gone. What TGR actually changes is four numbers, merged three-way against vanilla; what Victorian Century changes is much bigger (nation-specific noble trait sets, three new eligible pop types, ideology-switch blocks, China-officer bonuses). Both are folded into HC's bodies, sub-block by sub-block, three-way against vanilla. Full write-up, including a scope-safety bug found and fixed along the way, in `_HC+GoB+MoH/hc+tgr done/README.md`.

Same file also cleans up Mandate of Heaven's copy of `ig_rural_folk` — see "Foreign bugs found" below.

### China, the Ottomans and the USA: Victorian Century checked against all three history files

`chi - china.txt`, `tur - ottoman empire.txt` and `usa - usa.txt` are each fought over by up to three mods (TGR's starting companies, MoH/GoB/HC's own history, and now Victorian Century). China gets no VC content added (MoH's law choices win outright, for reasons spelled out in that file's own banner). The Ottomans and the USA both get safe VC additions layered on top: one new law plus two amendments for the Ottomans, twelve of Victorian Century's fourteen Bill-of-Rights-style amendments for the USA. Full write-up in `_HC+GoB+MoH/hc+tgr done/README.md`.

### Fifteen American character templates, Polk's DNA, Chinese dynamic names / flags, and the opium buttons

Victorian Century also rewrites 15 of Hail, Columbia!'s own character templates, shares a DNA file with HC for James K. Polk, and competes with Mandate of Heaven over Chinese dynamic country names, flag definitions and the two opium-decision buttons. Every one of these is resolved case by case — ten of the fifteen character templates are a clean adoption of VC's traits, five needed an editorial call recorded in a spreadsheet; Polk keeps both mods' DNA genes; the dynamic names and flags are unioned; the opium buttons keep Mandate of Heaven's richer body with three things folded in from VC. Full write-up in `_HC+GoB+MoH/hc+vc done/README.md`.

### Smaller merges

* **Gaudí's capital expansion, level 4.** Gates of the Bosphorus ships a stub of the modifier with nothing in it but an icon, so its Byzantium journal entry resolves without Morgenröte. GoB loads later, so with both mods the modifier loses all eight of its state modifiers. Morgenröte's body is re-issued.
* **The achievements screen.** `common/achievement_groups.txt` has no top-level keys, so it can only be overridden by path and the last mod takes the whole file. Mandate of Heaven is last, which drops Morgenröte's twelve achievement groups — roughly sixty achievements — and `achievement_exactly_100`, which MoH's copy of the vanilla file predates. Merged.
* **One Olympics instead of two.** Morgenröte hides the vanilla `revive_olympic_games_decision` because it replaces it with a whole mechanic; Gates of the Bosphorus rewrites it into a Greek decision and loads later. Morgenröte's suppression is re-issued — Greece loses one flavour decision, Morgenröte's Olympics stay. *This is a design call, not a conflict fix.*
* **Walter Chrysler and Mark Twain** keep Hail, Columbia!'s jobs (Basic Motors executive, Missouri agitator) and get Morgenröte's roles and `on_created` back on top.
* **Jacksonian Democrats** get TGR's `lawgroup_election_system` / `lawgroup_legislative_process` stances back, and Victorian Century's eight law-group stances too (two folded into HC's existing blocks, six appended whole).
* **Two starting companies.** TGR's Ong Lung Sheng Tea Company and Imperial Arsenal, lost when MoH and GoB win the Chinese and Ottoman history files by path.

## Foreign bugs found while building this

Worth sending to the authors.

* **Gates of the Bosphorus** — `common/static_modifiers/zz_gbbf_placeholder_modifiers.txt`. The stub is there so `has_modifier = gaudi_capital_expansion_level_4_modifier` resolves without Morgenröte, but GoB loads after Morgenröte, so the stub wins and blanks the real modifier. A `TRY_REPLACE_OR_CREATE` guarded on `morgenrote_is_active = no` would do the same job without the side effect.
* **Mandate of Heaven** — `common/interest_groups/moh_rural_folk.txt`. Of the fifteen sub-blocks it injects, two are its own (the KMT leader ideology, the Nongmin rename). The other thirteen are a copy of a pre-1.13 vanilla body: `has_law` where 1.13 has `has_law_or_variant`, strict scopes where 1.13 has `?=`, an **empty** `on_character_ig_membership` where vanilla has the Zanzibar religion rule, a `priority_cultures` missing vanilla's Zanzibar rule, and `commander_leader_chance`, which 1.13 renamed to `commander_leader_weight`. Injecting only the two real additions would be both smaller and safer.
* **Mandate of Heaven** — `common/achievement_groups.txt` is missing `achievement_exactly_100`, which current vanilla lists.
* **The Great Revision** — `common/journal_entries/TGR_ADJUSTMENTS_oregon.txt` is a copy of an older vanilla: pre-`sr:` region syntax, strict scopes instead of `?=`, a missing British Columbia homeland block, and `months = normal_modifier_time`, which is 152 years rather than five. Only two lines in the file are TGR's own. (Nothing to fix in this addon — Hail, Columbia! wins the path anyway.)
* **The Great Revision and Victorian Century**, independently — both downgrade `scope:interest_group ?= {` to the unsafe `= {` in the exact same `LEADER_POPULARITY` block of `ig_landowners`/`ig_rural_folk`. Restored to the safe form throughout in this addon (see the interest-group section above).

## Checked and deliberately not touched

* **Hail, Columbia! × E&F + Hotfix, × Power Blocs Expanded, × Private Sector Construction** — no shared keys and no shared paths. The seven localization keys HC shares with E&F all go to E&F, which loads earlier, and in localization the first file to define a key wins. **This addon therefore works unchanged with the `no e&f+psc` megapack variant.**
* **manifest_destiny** — Hail, Columbia! ships the file with the whole decision commented out: it removes the vanilla decision on purpose and replaces it with an 1100-line journal chain. TGR's rework of that decision goes with the file, and `great_revision_events.5` becomes unreachable. Victorian Century independently suppresses the same vanilla decision too (an 11-byte stub). Restoring any of them would give the player two or three Manifest Destinies.
* **je_oregon / je_conquer_oregon** — the only thing of TGR's that HC's rewrite drops is a journal-entry-group reassignment, and HC owns that group.
* **ig_armed_forces, ig_intelligentsia, ig_petty_bourgeoisie, ideology_communist, je_autocracy, state_trait_columbia_river.** In every one of these the last mod to touch the entry injects, or Victorian Century's much larger body simply doesn't occupy the sub-block HC/MoH touch. Additive, confirmed line by line.
* **law_ethnostate, law_freedom_of_conscience, law_protectionism, law_canton_system, law_theocracy.** In every case the last mod to touch the law injects, and into a sub-block nobody else names.
* **NAI** — defines merge per key; `NUM_GROWING_COLONIES_MAX` is set by Hail, Columbia! alone.
* **CHI_minguo (coat_of_arms)** — MoH's and Victorian Century's bodies are byte-identical after normalization; whichever wins the path, the picture is the same.
* **chi_daoguang_template** — Victorian Century `REPLACE_OR_CREATE:`s it early (adds one trait), Mandate of Heaven `INJECT:`s an unrelated `on_created` late. Load order alone makes them stack correctly; no compatch needed.
* **map_data/state_regions: STATE_GANSU, STATE_GUANGDONG, STATE_HINGGAN** — Mandate of Heaven ships the whole vanilla East-Asia region as a bare body and, with Victorian Century loading earlier in the order above, wins these three states automatically. No compatch needed, flagged here only so a build with a different load order doesn't assume this still holds.
* **Twenty character portraits** — GoB and HC carry their own copies of Morgenröte `dna_data` files. They win by load order and the differences are portrait genes; deliberate variants, not stale copies.
* **Goods** — the addon adds none. The set stays at 74 of the 128 ceiling.
* **Event ids** — no collisions in any of the four pairs.

## Playing without Victorian Century

Victorian Century is optional, but two of its layers are welded into files that another compatch also needs, so the trim uses two different mechanisms depending on the file. Every affected file says which one applies to it, right at the top, in a `### ===` banner — you don't need this table to find them, just open `common/` and skim for that marker.

**Six files exist only because of Victorian Century.** No other compatch in this addon needs them — just delete them.

| delete from `common/` |
| --- |
| `character_templates/zz_hcvc_character_templates.txt` |
| `dna_data/ecchi_usa_polk.txt` |
| `dynamic_country_names/zz_hcvc_dynamic_country_names.txt` |
| `flag_definitions/zz_hcvc_flag_definitions_chi.txt` |
| `scripted_buttons/zz_hcvc_opium_buttons.txt` |
| `history/countries/usa - usa.txt` |

(`usa - usa.txt` lives in the same physical folder as the TGR-shared history files below, but The Great Revision never touches this particular path — it's pure HC + VC, so deleting it just lets Hail, Columbia!'s own file win by load order.)

**Three files carry a Victorian Century layer welded on top of content another compatch also needs.** These ship in pairs: the active `.txt` (with VC's layer) and an inert `.txt.off` twin next to it (without VC's layer — Victoria 3 ignores files whose name doesn't end in a recognized extension, so the `.off` twin does nothing until renamed). To drop VC: delete the active file, then remove the `.off` suffix from its twin.

| delete this (has VC) | then enable this (no VC) |
| --- | --- |
| `interest_groups/zz_hct_ig_landowners.txt` | `interest_groups/zz_hct_ig_landowners.txt.off` → remove `.off` |
| `interest_groups/zz_hct_ig_rural_folk.txt` | `interest_groups/zz_hct_ig_rural_folk.txt.off` → remove `.off` |
| `history/countries/tur - ottoman empire.txt` | `history/countries/tur - ottoman empire.txt.off` → remove `.off` |

**One file needs no change at all:** `history/countries/chi - china.txt`. Victorian Century was checked against it line by line and nothing from VC turned out to be safe to add (see that file's own banner) — its content is identical whether or not you run VC.

If you installed the addon from the Workshop, the launcher puts deleted/renamed files back on the next update. Copying the mod folder into your own `Documents/Paradox Interactive/Victoria 3/mod/` and enabling that copy instead makes the trim stick.

## Running it without one of the other three mods

Every file here belongs to exactly one pair, so trimming the addon is a matter of deleting files — there is no separate download. Delete what the table says from the addon's `common/` folder and nothing else changes.

The three mods of the block itself — **Hail, Columbia!**, **Gates of the Bosphorus** and **Mandate of Heaven** — are not optional. Several files carry one of those mods' own bodies with the other side's changes merged into it, so without them the addon would be installing content from a mod you do not have.

| you don't have | delete from `common/` | what you lose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Morgenröte** | `achievement_groups.txt`<br>`character_templates/zz_hcm_characters.txt`<br>`decisions/zz_hcm_olympic_games.txt`<br>`static_modifiers/zz_hcm_gaudi_modifier.txt` | nothing. All four exist only to put Morgenröte's content back |
| **The Great Revision** | `ideologies/zz_hct_jacksonian_democrat.txt`<br>`history/countries/chi - china.txt`<br>`history/countries/tur - ottoman empire.txt` (and its `.off` twin)<br>`ai_strategies/zz_hctr_tgr_default_strategy.txt`<br>*and, if you also don't use Victorian Century:* `interest_groups/zz_hct_ig_landowners.txt.off`, `zz_hct_ig_rural_folk.txt.off` | nothing |
| **Kuromi's AI** | `ai_strategies/zz_hctr_ai_strategy_default.txt`<br>`ai_strategies/zz_hctr_tgr_default_strategy.txt` | the one case where something goes. The second file is built on the body in the first, so it has to go with it, and The Great Revision's injections into `ai_strategy_default` then stay dropped under Mandate of Heaven. Everything else in the addon is unaffected |
| **Victorian Century** | see "Playing without Victorian Century" above | nothing not documented there |

The megapack variants follow from the same table: for **no TGR** delete the four Great Revision files (plus the two `.off` twins if you also skip VC); for **no E&F + PSC** change nothing — this addon shares no key and no path with either of those.

## Rebuilding

```
python3 tools/regen_addon1.py --root <vic3_mods_out> --out <vic3_mods>
python3 tools/regen_hc_vc.py
python3 tools/build_addon1.py --repo <vic3_mods>
```

Both generators support `--check` to report drift without writing. Either one stops rather than guessing whenever a foreign mod moves a line one of these merges depends on — which is the only warning available, since none of these conflicts produce a single line in `error.log`.
