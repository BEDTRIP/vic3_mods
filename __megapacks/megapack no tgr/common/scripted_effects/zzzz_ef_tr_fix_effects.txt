### E&F + Tech & Res ComPatch Fix (Vic3 1.13)
### The "private ownership -> stocks" switch for buildings the compatch leaves
### stuck.
###
### Three separate problems, one effect:
###
### 1. building_consumer_electronics_industry. The compatch's block inside
###    private_ownership_production_stocks is the only one of its 168 that is
###    written wrong -- the ">= half" branch reads
###
###        is_production_method_active = pm_private_ownership_majority_manufacture_stock
###        activate_production_method  = pm_private_ownership_majority_manufacture_stock
###
###    i.e. "if it is already majority, set it to majority". Every other block
###    tests pm_no_private_ownership_manufacture_stock there. The building can
###    therefore never leave the "no stock" method.
###
###    05.09.2026 -- and once this file DID move it there, that block became a
###    hang. private_ownership_production_stocks runs from
###    on_production_method_changed, so every activate_production_method re-enters
###    it; the 167 correct blocks terminate because one flip clears their
###    condition, this one sets the very method it tests and never clears.
###    Reported from the wild as a repeatable stack-overflow CTD. The block is now
###    corrected at the source, in the pinned copy of the effect itself
###    (tools/regen_ef_tr_copies.py, fix_po_stocks), so the pair below is a no-op
###    belt-and-braces -- keep it, it is what covers a regen against a compatch
###    whose typo the author has meanwhile changed rather than fixed.
###
### 2. building_computer_assembly_plant. New in T&R 13.05.2026, absent from the
###    compatch entirely.
###
### 3. The ten vanilla industries the compatch renamed into their plural
###    `aliases` form (building_textile_mills, building_shipyards, ...). Nine of
###    those aliases are declared by vanilla and may well resolve in
###    has_building / b: / is_production_method_active; building_artillery_foundries
###    never existed at all. Rather than guess whether aliases resolve in script
###    scopes, this effect repeats the switch under the canonical names. If the
###    aliases do work, every block here is a no-op -- the condition cannot match,
###    because the compatch already put the building on the right method. If they
###    do not, this is what makes the mechanic work.
###
### Why an on_action and not an override of private_ownership_production_stocks:
### that effect is 82 KB and is itself a pinned copy of E&F's. Overriding it
### would mean re-syncing 82 KB after every E&F update to fix twelve blocks.
### on_actions are additive, so this file overrides nothing. Wired up in
### common/on_actions/ and called once at game start from common/history/global/.
###
### All twelve buildings use the manufacture stock pair -- checked against the
### compatch's own blocks, which is also where the plural names came from.
