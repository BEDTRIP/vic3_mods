# ComPatch: The Great Revision + Victorian Century

**Game 1.13 (exe 1.13.11). The Great Revision 2.0, Victorian Century as unpacked 2026-08-25 (the mod declares no version).**

Victorian Century loads after The Great Revision and re-declares a great deal of what TGR had already rewritten. Nothing errors, nothing is logged, and TGR quietly stops doing about half of what it was installed for. This patch puts TGR's work back on top of VC's, record by record.

## Load order

1. The Great Revision (+ its Russian translation if you use it)
2. …the rest of your set…
3. Victorian Century (+ Victorian Century [RU])
4. **this ComPatch**

**This patch must load after Victorian Century.** That is the whole point: VC is the mod doing the overwriting.

## What was being lost

172 shared keys and 14 shared file paths. VC wins every one of them, because it loads last and because it uses full bodies (`REPLACE_OR_CREATE:` or bare) almost everywhere.

| what | what disappeared |
| --- | --- |
| **the eight interest groups** | TGR's whole IG rework: the `0.030` membership multiplier (vanilla 0.0025), the raised base weights, the `law_appointed_bureaucrats` bonus for academics and bureaucrats, the `law_dedicated_police` bonus for soldiers and officers, the trade-union multipliers for egalitarianism / labor movement / socialism |
| **99 buy packages** | TGR's consumption rebalance — "+25% on basic food, luxury food and luxury drinks from wealth 10, +50% from wealth 21", plus his intoxicants and stimulants curve |
| **twelve companies** | TGR's `ai_weight = 9999` (which is what makes the AI actually found flavoured companies), his relaxed prerequisites, his rewritten `building_types` |
| **German unification** | TGR's entire rework: two scripted buttons, the monthly pulse firing the Bismarck event, the hidden effect annexing 21 German states, the `great_revision_german_unification.6` broadcast. All of it uncalled |
| **eight pop needs** | TGR's supply-share system — narrow the ceiling on any one good, raise the floor, so pops have to consume a mix |
| **`base_values`** | loan interest, +100 bureaucracy / authority / influence, officer and soldier political strength, company construction efficiency, trade advantage |
| **eight country histories** | TGR's five historical companies (Gebrüder Thonet, Ong Lung Sheng, Mitsui, Philips, Imperial Arsenal), his Spanish and French starting-law reworks, his British adjustments |

## How the merge is made

Vanilla 1.13.11 is the common ancestor of both mods for every record here, so this is a plain three-way merge, generated by `tools/regen_vc_tgr.py` rather than written out by hand. What the two authors did to different lines is kept from both. What they did to the same lines is a conflict, and every conflict is either settled by a rule below or printed by the generator for a human to read.

Where both mods carry the same non-vanilla text — and they do, in the parties and low buy packages, because both picked up the same third-party base — nothing is merged, because there is nothing in dispute.

## The three decisions

**Buy packages — VC's numbers, TGR's multipliers.** VC rebalanced services, leisure, communication, luxury items and free movement; TGR rebalanced food and drink. Where only one of them moved a cell off vanilla, that mod's number is used. Where both did, and the good is one of the three TGR's own file header names, VC's number is taken and TGR's multiplier applied to it. Both rebalances survive. The multiplier is derived rather than copied, so it follows VC if VC rebalances again.

**German unification — stitched, not decided.** VC's explicit 31-state list and its Frankfurt-parliament gate stay (a Greater German formation including Bohemia and Austria remains possible). TGR's formation rules, buttons, Bismarck pulse and 21-state annexation come back on top. TGR's `use_culture_states = yes` is dropped: with VC's state list present it would be the formable deciding its own territory twice.

**Parties — VC keeps three-interest-group coalitions.** TGR zeroes the multiplier for a party's third and later member ("maximum 2 IGs per party"); VC splits it into an expensive third member and a worse fourth. VC's version stands. TGR's other two party adjustments are kept.

## Ideology stances — the one part that is not a merge

Everything above puts back something one of the two authors wrote. This part is different: it is content neither of them wrote, because neither could.

The Great Revision adds 13 law groups and 60 laws. Victorian Century adds 56 ideologies and 10 laws. **Not one of VC's ideologies had an opinion on any of TGR's laws, and not one of TGR's ideologies had an opinion on any of VC's.** An interest group led by VC flavour was neutral on TGR's entire political layer — it would not push for it, would not resist it, and the political half of TGR simply stopped mattering wherever VC's characters ended up in charge.

Nothing in either mod lets that be inferred. Picking the "nearest" existing ideology and copying its stances was tried and rejected: the closest match agrees with its donor on only 25–50% of the laws they both name, and the donor itself covers 1–2 of the 13 groups. So the stances here were placed by hand, 1725 of them, and the workbook they were placed in ships beside this file (`vc_tgr_ideology_grid.xlsx`). Edit it and re-run the generator to change them.

Two files, one per direction, so a set that drops one mod can drop one file:

* `zz_vc_tgr_stances_on_tgr_laws.txt` — 56 VC ideologies, 1245 stances on TGR's laws;
* `zz_vc_tgr_stances_on_vc_laws.txt` — 48 TGR ideologies, 480 stances on VC's laws.

Both use `INJECT:`, not a full body: the point is to add a law group an ideology never named. Named entries inside an injected sub-block override same-named ones and new ones are appended, so injecting `lawgroup_taxation = { law_askeri_tax = approve }` into an ideology that already declares `lawgroup_taxation` adds that one line and disturbs nothing else.

**One stance does not survive a set with Hail, Columbia!**: `ideology_jacksonian_democrat` is re-declared by HC with a full body further down the chain, so its opinions on VC's laws are eaten. They belong in the HC addon instead. The generator asserts this rather than trusting a comment — if HC ever stops re-declaring it, the run says so.

## What is NOT in this patch, and why

* **`common/buildings` (17 industrial buildings)** — VC only `TRY_INJECT:`s a `can_build_private` sub-block nobody else names. TGR's bodies survive intact.
* **`common/technology/technologies` (6)** — both sides only inject, into different sub-blocks or different modifier keys. Nothing overlaps.
* **`common/production_methods` (3 railway methods)** — VC injects an `unscaled` block into TGR's `building_modifiers`, which already names `workforce_scaled` and `level_scaled`. Three different named sub-blocks, they merge.
* **`common/defines`** — the four shared groups (`NAI`, `NCharacters`, `NEconomy`, `NPops`) share **zero** inner keys. Defines merge per key, so the group names overlapping means nothing.
* **`common/decisions/manifest_destiny.txt`** — VC replaces it with an 11-byte `#nothing` and kills TGR's version. Not restored **on purpose**: Hail, Columbia! comes later in the chain and kills Manifest Destiny again, replacing it with an 1100-line journal chain. If you are not running Hail, Columbia!, TGR's Manifest Destiny stays dead and this patch does not bring it back.
* **Localization, event ids, GUI widget names** — zero collisions of any kind.
* **`common/goods`** — VC has no such folder. The 128-goods ceiling is untouched by this pair.

## Notes

* **Maintenance.** Every file is generated. After either mod updates, re-run `tools/regen_vc_tgr.py` and read the conflict list it prints: a conflict that was not there before means the two authors have started fighting over a line they used to leave alone. `--check` reports drift without writing.
* **`common/history/countries/*` and `common/decisions/canada_australia.txt` are shipped at the vanilla paths**, because both mods overlap there by path. A second file with the same `COUNTRIES` container would add rather than replace, and every country would get its companies and journal entries twice.
* **`chi - china.txt` and `tur - ottoman empire.txt` are shadowed** if you also run my Hail Columbia + Gates of the Bosphorus + Mandate of Heaven addon, which owns those paths. That is intended; the files here are for sets without it.
* Victorian Century declares an empty `id`, so it cannot be named in `relationships` — only in the description and in `tested_with`.
* This is a **compatibility patch**, not a rebalance — with one declared exception: the ideology stances above are authored, because neither mod contains anything to derive them from. Every other number in it comes from one of the two mods.
