### E&F Hotfix — currency laws: typo removed, selection constrained
###
### WHAT IT WAS. All 95 currency laws required
###
###     unlocking_technologies = { currency_standars }
###
### No technology by that name exists — the real one is currency_standards,
### with a "d". One letter, 95 times. Because of it the whole
### lawgroup_currency_type was unavailable to EVERYONE, FOREVER.
###
### It did NOT block the intended path: introduction_of_<currency> calls
### activate_law directly, which bypasses requirements. The typo only closed
### off manual selection by the player.
###
### WHY IT IS WORTH FIXING. One case: a country formed mid-game. Germany
### inherits Prussia's central bank, Prussia researched central_banking long
### ago, so on_researched will never fire again — GER could never get the mark
### by history (it has none), by automation (already spent) or by law (typo).
### An open law group is the only way out.
###
### THE CONSTRAINT. Since the laws can now be enacted by hand, they need a
### possible block — otherwise France starts printing pounds sterling. E&F had
### none at all: not one possible across 95 laws. The author never intended
### "one currency per country" either — he deliberately hands the same currency
### to several tags (Canadian dollar to CAN, ONT and QUE; Prussian thaler to
### PRU and NGF; Bolivian peso to BOL and PBC).
###
### So possible is built from his own data:
###
###   * a law is available to the tags E&F itself hands that currency to in
###     common/history/global/99_ef_history_global_variable.txt — including the
###     Historical Map Mod branch, so formable countries are covered too;
###   * ownerless currencies (no tag behind them in history) are open to
###     everyone — a reserve for released nations;
###   * currencies whose good is commented out (by the author, or by us for
###     the 128 goods limit) are shut outright: holding a law with no good
###     behind it is worse than having no currency, the bank mints nothing and
###     the production methods point at nothing.
###
###   * and every available law additionally requires
###     has_modifier = has_central_bank. This matters: you cannot pick a
###     currency before you have a central bank. Otherwise a Japan player would
###     take the yen in 1836 and skip the entire Meiji chain that gates it.
###
### MAINTENANCE: this file overrides common/laws/01_ef_currency_type.txt in
### full. Rebuild it with the generator after an E&F update (see README).
###
