### E&F's market_goods_is_currency (common/scripted_triggers/00_ef_custom_trigger.txt) -- hand-maintained.
###
### E&F's menus use it to keep currency goods out of the ordinary market lists. Since Д2.1 (5.10.2026) the only
### one left, liquidity_currency, is settlement services -- an ordinary good, so nothing is a currency any more.
### (Before: OR = { goods = g:liquidity_currency }; E&F's 95-good list logged ~12,000 errors in two seconds.)
###
### REPLACE_OR_CREATE: is what makes this override at all.

REPLACE_OR_CREATE:market_goods_is_currency = {
	always = no
}
