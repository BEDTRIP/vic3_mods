### E&F Hotfix — who gets the recomputed local_currency output
###
### The same condition is needed in two places (game start and the monthly
### pulse), so it lives here.
###
########################################################################
### No market throttle needed anymore
###
### This used to gate the formula to countries NOT sharing a market with a
### real currency: local_currency was tradeable, so a cheap flat grant could
### leak onto a shared market and undercut real national currencies there.
### That is why an earlier version of this trigger walked
### market ?= { owner ?= { ... } } to check who else was on the market.
###
### E&F's own update (02.09.2026) made local_currency non-tradeable at the
### source. A non-tradeable good cannot reach any market, shared or not, so
### the leak this trigger throttled is now impossible by construction — the
### formula can apply to everyone with no_money_production, unconditionally.
########################################################################

zz_ef_local_currency_needs_throttle = {
	has_modifier = no_money_production
}
