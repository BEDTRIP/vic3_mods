# EF.48, В2.3 (decided 2.10): gold mines and fields mint nothing into the
# treasury. Vanilla gives country_minting_add per workforce to every gold
# method (production_methods/03_mines.txt); on top of it our central bank
# coins half of the gold mined (zz_ef_mint_step: metal to the reserve, money
# to its owners, a share to the treasury -- zz_ef_mint_treasury_share) and
# the gold is sold on the market in full -- the same ounce counted three
# times. Issue goes through the central bank now.
#
# Counterweights, not REPLACE: repeated keys in a modifier block add up
# (rules, section «Префиксы БД»), so -N cancels vanilla's N and the rest of
# each method stays as vanilla / other mods have it.
# !! MAINTENANCE !! The numbers are vanilla's (1.13): re-check against
# 03_mines.txt after a game patch. A later mod restating one of these methods
# in full (T&R's compatch did so for the diesel pump) drops the counterweight
# and brings its own minting -- T&R is out of the set since 25.08.2026.
#############################################
