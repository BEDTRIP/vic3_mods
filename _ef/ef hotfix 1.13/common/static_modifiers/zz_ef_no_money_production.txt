### E&F Hotfix — the flat 2500 is killed at the source
###
### E&F puts the no_money_production modifier on every country without a
### monetary system, and that modifier carries
### state_sell_orders_local_currency_add = 2500. It is a country modifier, so
### the 2500 lands in EVERY state the country owns, with no regard for
### population, wealth or anything else. Roughly 600 of the game's 724
### countries have no monetary system.
###
### REPLACE: overwrites the whole entry and leaves nothing but the icon. The
### entry stays alive, so every has_modifier = no_money_production check in
### E&F keeps working exactly as before — it just cannot print money anymore.
###
### Why at the source rather than by subtracting it back:
###
### 1. Timing. E&F applies no_money_production from the law_no_monetary_system
###    effect on its own pulse, while we recalculated on ours, and the game
###    spreads on_monthly_pulse_country across the days of the month. Up to a
###    month could pass between the two, and the state printed the full 2500
###    the whole time. That is what showed up in game as "2.5k keeps popping
###    up on random vassals". Countries born mid-game — revolts, releases,
###    unifications — started life with the full amount.
###
### 2. A ceiling. If the computed demand came out above 2500 per state there
###    was nothing left to subtract, and the country kept E&F's number. The
###    replacement grant has no upper bound beyond a sanity cap.
###
### What replaces it: see zz_ef_local_currency_fix.txt and common/on_actions/.

REPLACE:no_money_production = {
	icon = gfx/interface/icons/timed_modifier_icons/no_money_production.dds
}
