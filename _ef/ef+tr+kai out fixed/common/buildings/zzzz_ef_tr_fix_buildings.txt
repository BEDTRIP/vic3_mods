### E&F + Tech & Res ComPatch Fix (Vic3 1.13)
### Buildings the compatch misses or names wrong.
###
### Everything in this file is INJECT:, so nothing T&R or the compatch defines
### is overwritten -- the PM groups are appended to whatever list survives.
### The three buildings that DO need a full re-statement are rebuilt straight
### into the compatch's own zztr_vanilla_buildings.txt.

### Gold mines are NOT here any more, and that is the point of this mod being a
### replacement rather than a patch. The compatch writes
### INJECT:building_gold_mines -- plural, no such building -- so gold mines never
### got T&R's data layer, and a plain INJECT: of a bad key costs one line in
### error.log on every load. Layered on top, all we could do was add the right
### key and leave the wrong one logging. Carrying the compatch's own file, the
### key is simply corrected in place; see zzef_vanilla_mines.txt and COPY_FIXUPS
### in tools/regen_ef_tr_copies.py.
###
### building_gold_field is deliberately left alone: the compatch author patched
### silver and gold mines only, and T&R itself puts pmg_data_optimization_* into
### 30 vanilla buildings without touching any precious-metal mine. Adding the
### group to gold fields would be a design decision, not a repair.

### Two T&R industries the compatch never wires into E&F.
###
### building_computer_assembly_plant is new in Tech & Res 13.05.2026 and appears
### nowhere in the compatch -- not in the building injections, not in the
### ownership effect, not in the crisis lists.
###
### building_consumer_electronics_industry has existed since the 1.12 build. The
### compatch lists it in private_ownership_production_stocks and in both crisis
### effects, but never gives it the PM groups those effects switch, so
### activate_production_method there points at a method the building does not
### have. (The switch itself is also miswritten; see
### zzzz_ef_tr_fix_effects.txt.)
###
### Both are bg_heavy_industry with ownership_type = self, exactly like the 33
### T&R buildings the compatch does cover. The three T&R buildings that stay
### uncovered are correct as they are: building_research_center has
### can_build_private = always no, building_modern_state_baseline is
### buildable = no, and building_nuclear_weapons_silo is government-only --
### E&F does not put liquidity into government buildings.
INJECT:building_computer_assembly_plant = {
	production_method_groups = {
		pmg_market_liquidity #E&F
		pmg_private_ownership_manufacture_stock
	}
}

INJECT:building_consumer_electronics_industry = {
	production_method_groups = {
		pmg_market_liquidity #E&F
		pmg_private_ownership_manufacture_stock
	}
}
